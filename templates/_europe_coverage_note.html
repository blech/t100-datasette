These are Eurostat's and the UK Civil Aviation Authority's figures for European airports, not T-100's. The airports and countries reporting grow over the years (Eurostat's early years are thin, and Turkey's passengers start in 2012), so a total's rise is partly the data's coverage. A route between two reporting airports is reported by both and counted once, from one airport's report. More on the European data.
